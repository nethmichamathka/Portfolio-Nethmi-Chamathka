User Story

Online Vehicle Registration Renewal

As a vehicle owner, I want to renew my registration online, so that I don't have to visit the office.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given a valid registration number, when the owner submits the renewal form, then the system shows the fee and due date.
  • Given a successful payment, then a digital receipt is emailed and the status changes to "Renewed".
  • Given an expired insurance record, then renewal is blocked with a clear error message.
AS-IS vs TO-BE

Registration Renewal Process

AS-IS (manual)

  1. Visit the office
  2. Queue and fill a paper form
  3. Clerk verifies documents
  4. Pay at the counter
  5. Collect the certificate

TO-BE (system)

  1. Log in online
  2. Auto-fetch vehicle record
  3. System validates documents
  4. Pay online
  5. Download the certificate
